## Acceptance obligations

evals/book-review-klinkenborg.cases.json maps every new card to recognition,
execution alternatives and a restraint case. Tests verify card identities,
source locators, retrieval and complete exception transport. They do not execute
or certify host-model editorial judgment. The existing forty concept IDs remain
stable. Before claiming complete idea coverage for this source, an independent
review must challenge this inventory for omissions and adjudicate transfer to
unseen passages. Other books remain substantially unreviewed.
