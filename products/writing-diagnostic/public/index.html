Has the writing earned what it says?

Writing Diagnostic reviews the thought behind the prose: what was noticed, what a word refers to, what supports a claim, and what a sentence does for its reader.

  • Violation: the wording fails in the available context.
  • Pressure test: context may justify it. Interrogate before editing.
  • Pass: the language earns its place.

Inspect a finding, answer the thinking question, then scan wording options. Keeping the original is a valid result.
