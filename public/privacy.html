Writing Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Writing Assistant is a writing and editing service operated by Matthew Lane.

Data processed

When you use Writing Assistant, the service receives the text and context you choose to send. This can include drafts, notes, source material, audience and purpose, and optional voice samples.

The hosted service sends the material needed to complete the request to OpenAI so the bounded writing pipeline can plan, write, audit, and, when needed, repair the result.

Storage and logging

The application does not intentionally persist user-submitted drafts or generated results in its own application database. Pipeline model calls are configured with storage disabled. Sensitive draft and output text are not intentionally attached to application traces.

Infrastructure providers may process ordinary operational data needed to deliver and secure the service, such as request timing, network metadata, and error information.

No sale of personal data

Writing Assistant does not sell user drafts or personal data and does not use submitted writing for advertising.

Service providers

OpenAI processes model requests used by the writing pipeline. Railway hosts the service and can process network and operational data needed to deliver it. Their handling of data is governed by their applicable policies and terms.

Your choices

Do not submit information you do not want processed by Writing Assistant and its service providers. Optional voice samples and source context can be omitted.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Writing Assistant support page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
